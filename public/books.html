About the Book

A comprehensive reference book exploring the integration of Tiny Machine Learning (TinyML), Internet of Medical Things (IoMT), Embedded Artificial Intelligence, Biomedical Sensors, and Edge Computing for next-generation intelligent healthcare systems.

Book Overview

This edited book focuses on intelligent healthcare solutions using Tiny Machine Learning (TinyML), IoT, Edge AI, Biomedical Sensors, Wearable Devices, Federated Learning, Blockchain, and Embedded Systems for early disease diagnosis. It bridges academia, industry, and healthcare by presenting practical applications, research methodologies, clinical implementations, and future research directions.
